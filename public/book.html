Preface

This volume records communications by Taylor Heller and Monsters Ink LLC, and the replies retrieved for those communications. It does not assess technical merit, and it does not report an award, a license, or a patent grant.

Four labels are used. Closed: supported by a retrieved letter, receipt, or screenshot. Stated: asserted by Taylor, with no matching receipt in this review. Open: no closing document. Refused: written in a source, and not supported by the remaining record.

Application 19/658,750 is the nonprovisional named on the 27 April 2026 filing receipt. The March messages use the name ARES-UCX for a patent-pending suspension. The two names are kept as they appear. MAGPIE, where used, means application 19/658,750.

Street addresses, telephone numbers, and card numbers are omitted. Application numbers are retained because they appear on the receipts.
